How the model and AI work
  • Data. Fixtures, live scores, results, league tables and club crests come from football-data.org through this app's server, which refreshes them every minute while games are on.
  • Team strength. Each club gets home and away attack and defence ratings from goals scored and conceded, compared with the league average and shrunk toward average early in the season. Cup ties use each club's domestic ratings, adjusted for league strength.
  • Conditions. Form, weather, pitch, crowd, altitude, rest days, missing players, referee style and derby status each multiply expected goals. The factor table shows every adjustment.
  • Markets. A Poisson model with a low-score correction gives a probability for every scoreline; 1X2, totals, both-teams-to-score, clean sheets and handicaps are summed from it. During a match it re-runs on the minutes left from the current score.
  • AI analyst. Claude receives the fixture, both clubs' records, form, the model's numbers, your conditions and your prices, and writes a verdict, key factors, markets worth a look and the risks. It works only from that data and can be wrong.

No model or AI can guarantee a result. Bet only what you can afford to lose, and set limits before you start.
